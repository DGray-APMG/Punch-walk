For construction project managers

How to use Punch Walk

Punch Walk runs the 7 Brew stand hand-over punch list in the field: mark every checklist item, document deficiencies with marked-up photos, send tasks to vendors, review their completion photos, and share reports. It works offline, and everything you record stays on your device until you share it.

The team passcode is sent to you separately. Never forward it to vendors. Vendors only use the stand number.

2. Install on your phone

Do this once, with signal.

  1. Open https://dgray-apmg.github.io/Punch-walk/. Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
  2. Add it to your home screen:
    • iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    • Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Punch Walk from the new home screen icon.
  4. Enter the team passcode and tap Unlock. The 7 Brew checklist loads and stays on your phone.

Always open the app from the home screen icon, not a browser tab. iPhone can clear data saved in a browser tab after a period of non-use; home screen apps keep their data.

On a computer

Open the app link in Chrome, Edge or Safari. To work on a walk started on your phone, see Move a walk to your computer.

3. Start a punch walk

  1. On the home screen, tap Start a new punch walk.
  2. Enter the stand number, address, walk date, general contractor, your name, and attendees.
  3. Tap Start walk. Every checklist item appears, grouped by location.

Enter the stand number exactly as it appears on the project, for example CIPM0169. It is the password vendors use to open their task files.

To change these details later, open the walk and tap the settings icon (sliders) at the top.

4. Mark items

Each item has three buttons on the right. Tap again to clear a status.

ButtonMeaning
✓Complete
✕Not complete (a punch item that needs work)
N/ADoes not apply to this stand

Find items fast

  • The chips at the top (All, Not checked, Not complete, Complete, N/A, With photos) filter by status. Swipe that row sideways to see them all.
  • Type in the search box to find an item by its words, number or your notes.
  • Tap Filter to pick a responsible party, division or location. You can pick as many as you like in each group: tap an option to add it, tap it again to remove it, or tap All to clear that group. Items show if they match any pick within a group and every group you used, for example GC or Low Voltage, in Plumbing or Electrical. The list updates as you tap; the bottom button shows how many items match. Tap it to close.
  • When filters are on, the button reads Filter (2) and a blue bar names everything that is filtered. Tap Clear all in that bar to reset every filter, status chip and search at once. A blue line under the progress bar shows how far along the filtered items are.
  • Previous and Next inside an item stay within your filters.

Item details

Tap an item's text to open it. There you can:

  • Write notes / corrective action: what is wrong, who fixes it, by when.
  • Change the responsible party (GC, Low Voltage, Sign Vendor and so on).
  • Change the division or location if the item belongs somewhere else. The item moves to that section of the list and reports use the new values.
  • Check Final approval given on completed items.
  • Add photos (see the next section).
  • Use Previous and Next to move through items without going back to the list.

Items not on the checklist

At the bottom of the list, tap Add an item not on the list. Enter the description, location, division and responsible party. Added items start as Not complete.

5. Photos and markup

Take photos

  1. In an item, tap Take photos. The first time, allow camera access.
  2. Tap the round shutter once for each photo. There is no limit and no "Use Photo" step between shots.
  3. Thumbnails line up along the bottom. Tap × on a thumbnail to remove it.
  4. Tap Done to add them all. A single photo opens straight into markup.

Other ways to add photos:

  • From library adds photos already on your phone (pick several at once).
  • Or use the phone's camera app opens the regular camera for one photo, when you need zoom, flash or HDR.

Mark up a photo

Tap any photo to open markup. Pick a color (red, yellow, white, blue), then a tool:

  • Arrow, Circle, Box: drag on the photo.
  • Freehand: draw with your finger.
  • Text (T): tap to place a text box or drag to size one, then type. The text shrinks to fit as you type more. Tap Done or tap outside the box to finish. To edit text later, pick the Text tool and tap the box.

Undo removes the last mark, Clear removes all marks, and Save keeps them. Marked-up photos appear in reports and exports; the original is kept too.

Delete a photo

Tap the trash icon on the photo, then tap Delete? to confirm.

6. Site photos

Site photos are general project photos for the record, such as elevations, equipment and finished areas. They are not punch items.

  1. In the walk, tap Site photos.
  2. Use Take photos or From library.
  3. Add an optional caption under each photo. Tap a photo to mark it up.
  4. To save them, tap Share or save as JPEG files. Each photo is its own JPEG, named with the stand, date, number and caption. On a phone, pick Save Images (Photos), Save to Files, Mail or Dropbox.

Site photos can also go into the PDF report (see the next section).

7. Reports and sharing

In the walk, tap Report.

Choose what to include

  • Quick buttons: All items, Open items (Not complete plus Not checked), Complete, N/A. You can also switch each status on or off.
  • Filter by responsible party, location, division, item number range, text in the item or notes, and items with photos or notes.
  • Sort and group by location, item number, responsible party, status or division.
  • Choose whether to include item photos and site photos in the PDF.

The count and preview show exactly what will be in the report. Your settings are remembered for each walk.

Build and send

  • Build PDF report: summary, counts by responsible party, each item with notes and photos, and site photos at the end.
  • Build Excel punch list: the filtered items with status and notes, ready to sort in Excel.
  • Share or save photos: the item photos in the report, marked-up and original.

Each file opens a panel. Tap Share and pick Mail to email it, or Dropbox, OneDrive or Files to save it. On a computer, use Save to this device.

Example: to send the GC their open items, tap Open items, check GC under Responsible party, and build the PDF. The file name includes "Open items - GC".

8. Move a walk to your computer

After the walk, you can manage the punch list from your computer instead of your phone.

  1. On the phone, open the walk, tap the settings icon, then Save full walk file.
  2. Share it to OneDrive, Dropbox or Files, or email it to yourself. The file holds everything: items, notes, photos, site photos and vendor updates.
  3. On the computer, open https://dgray-apmg.github.io/Punch-walk/, click Open a walk file, and choose the file.
  4. Keep working on the computer. The computer does not need the team passcode to open a walk file.

Once the file is saved somewhere safe, you can delete the walk from your phone. To move it back, save a new full walk file on the computer and open it on the phone.

If you open a file for a walk that is already on that device, the app asks: Replace the copy on this device (use the file's version) or Keep both.

Changes are not synced between devices. Work on one device at a time, and save a new walk file whenever you switch.

9. Vendor updates

Send a vendor their open items, let them upload completion photos and notes, then accept or deny each one. Vendors cannot change your punch list, and they never need the team passcode.

Send tasks to a vendor

  1. In the walk, tap Vendor updates.
  2. Pick the responsible party, choose which statuses to include, and choose whether to include your photos.
  3. Enter your email (it is remembered) so the vendor knows where to reply.
  4. Tap Build vendor task file and save or share it.
  5. Tap Copy instructions for the vendor, paste them into an email, and attach the task file.

What the vendor does

  1. Opens https://dgray-apmg.github.io/Punch-walk/vendor.html on a computer or phone.
  2. Loads the task file and enters the stand number as the password.
  3. Enters their name, company and email.
  4. For each task, adds completion photos (drag and drop works on a computer), adds notes, and checks Mark this task complete.
  5. If the file covers more than one responsible party, can tap one or more parties at the top of the list to show only those tasks.
  6. Clicks Create response file, saves it, and emails it back to you.

The vendor's work is saved in their browser, so they can stop and continue later.

Review a response

  1. Save the response file from the vendor's email (on iPhone: tap the attachment, Share, Save to Files).
  2. Open the walk, tap Vendor updates, then Load a vendor response file.
  3. Each update shows the vendor's notes and photos. Tap a photo to enlarge it.
    • Accept: the item becomes Complete, the vendor photos are added to it, and their note is added to yours.
    • Deny: enter a reason. The item stays Not complete. If the item is already Complete (for example accepted from another vendor), it stays Complete.
    • Undo reverses either choice, as long as the item has not changed since. If it has, change it on the item screen instead.
  4. Each vendor's response is kept separately and only touches the items that vendor updated, so loading one vendor's file never erases another's. An item that appears in more than one response is tagged Also in the response from….
  5. Tap Email vendor to open a drafted email listing what was accepted and what needs more work.

Items with an update waiting show "Vendor update to review" in the list, and the Vendor updates button shows a count. A response only loads into the walk it was sent from, and the same response cannot be loaded twice.

Stand numbers appear on plans and permits, so the stand number keeps out casual viewers but is not strong security. Send task files only to the responsible vendor.

10. Delete a walk

  1. Open the walk and tap the settings icon.
  2. Tap Delete this walk, then tap again to confirm.

This removes the walk, its notes, item photos, site photos and vendor photos from the app. It cannot be undone, so save a report or full walk file first.

Deleting does not remove photos you added from your phone's library (they stay in Photos), or files you already exported and shared.

11. Offline use and app updates

  • After the first unlock, the app opens and works with no signal: marking items, photos, markup, reports and walk files.
  • A "No signal" notice appears when you are offline. Your work keeps saving on the phone.
  • Sharing files to email or Dropbox needs signal at the moment you send.
  • When a new version is published, an Update bar appears at the top of the home screen. Tap it; your walks are kept.

12. Update the checklist

For whoever maintains the master punch list.

  1. In the app, open Template options on the home screen, under Update the team checklist.
  2. Enter a passcode (the current one or a new one) and choose the updated Excel workbook.
  3. Save the file it builds, template.enc.json.
  4. Upload it to the GitHub repo (Add file, Upload files), replacing the old file, and commit.

Phones pick up the new checklist the next time the app opens with signal. If the passcode changed, each phone asks for it once. Walks already started keep their original list.

To use a different checklist on one phone only, choose Load Excel for this phone under Template options.

13. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Take photos says camera access is blockediPhone: Settings, Safari, Camera, set to Ask or Allow, then reopen the app. You can also use "Or use the phone's camera app".
The checklist asks for the passcode againThe team checklist was updated with a new passcode. Ask your director for it. You can keep using the current list until then.
No Share button, only SaveThat browser cannot share files. Save the file, then attach it to an email or upload it.
A vendor says the stand number does not workCheck the stand number in Walk details matches what you gave them. Spaces and capital letters do not matter.
A vendor response will not loadOpen the walk the tasks were sent from. A response only loads into that walk.
A vendor's response file is too large to emailAsk them to send fewer photos per response, or split the tasks into two responses.
A walk is missing on a new phone or computerWalks live on the device where they were made. Move them with a full walk file.
